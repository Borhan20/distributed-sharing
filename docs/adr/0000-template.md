## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces and constraints matter? Keep it
factual. Link to the plan, spike reports and issues.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two short paragraphs. Use "we will".

## Consequences

- Good: what becomes easier.
- Bad: what becomes harder, and what risk we accept.
- Follow-up: work this decision creates.
